    01 — About

    A few words about me.

    I grew up in Cerritos, California and I'm now a freshman at UC Irvine studying Business Administration, on track to graduate in 2030. My focus is finance — markets, trading, and the math that drives them. Most mornings I read the QQQ options flow on InsiderFinance — net GEX, call GEX, put GEX — to set my daily bias, then trade the Nasdaq 100 futures (NQ) on funded prop firm capital around the cash open.

    My main project is a custom Pine Script indicator suite I built for TradingView. It overlays the context I want on screen — gamma walls, value areas, day-type detection, six trigger patterns — and scores setups so I know when to take them. I trade it live every day and refine it as I learn.

    Long-term, I'm aiming for a career in markets — quant, hedge funds, prop trading, or private equity. For now I'm focused on getting reps, reading the tape, shipping better tools, and finding an internship at any firm that touches the financial markets.
