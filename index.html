    001 Now

    NQ Context — Trading Indicator Suite

    A pair of custom Pine Script indicators I built and trade live every morning on NQ futures. NQ Context overlays gamma levels (0DTE, weekly, monthly walls and flips), confluence zones from prior-day value area and round numbers, a session volume profile with POC/VAH/VAL, initial-balance day-type classification, and a 6-pattern setup detector that scores trades from 1–10. The companion CVD + Divergence script tracks cumulative volume delta and auto-marks bull/bear divergences against price pivots. Both run on TradingView and feed alerts to my phone during the open.
